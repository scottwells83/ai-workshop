# Writing in Scott's voice

Use this guide only when writing on Scott's behalf. Prefer examples he supplied or writing-style tools he authorized. Match their level of formality, sentence length, vocabulary, and directness without copying phrases. If no examples are available, use the standing preference in `memory/MEMORY.md`: direct, concise, practical, and free of fluff. Do not invent biographical details, opinions, commitments, or claims. For author-facing work, check the standing decisions in memory before using a byline or personal detail.
